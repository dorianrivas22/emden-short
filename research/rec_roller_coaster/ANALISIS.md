## 1. Guion completo (Whisper large-v3)

> **He bent the roller coaster's loop out of shape.**
> You see, it's the first ride ever to go upside down, and it cost him everything.
> So of course, his rival copies it bolt for bolt.
> **But the night before opening,** his test dummy comes back crushed like a soda can.
> But he can't miss the opening, so he grabs a hammer and beats the bottom into a long, lazy curve.
> **Does he know what he's doing? Not really.**
> His rival laughs, then sneaks in and cranks the sides together until the loop is tall and skinny, so the first cart will snap like a twig.
> The next morning, the town lines up at the rival's perfect circle, and the rival climbs in front to show off.
> The owner climbs into his, and as his cart tips over the first hill, he finally sees the tall, skinny loop ahead.
> **But it's too late.**
> **You see,** a cart can creep around a hairpin turn at five, but take it at 60, and it flips. A cart is fastest at the bottom of a loop, and a perfect circle puts a hairpin right there, which is what crushed the dummy.
> But his hammer made the bottom gentle, and the rival's squeeze moved the hairpin up top, where the cart is crawling.
> **So he floats over without a scratch, just as the rival's cart hits the bottom of that perfect circle.**

**Claves de escritura**
- **El sabotaje del rival es lo que salva al héroe**: doble ironía. El héroe acierta por accidente ("Not really") y el villano completa la solución sin querer.

- **La explicación más larga de los 5** (15 s) con una **analogía de coche** antes de la física de la montaña rusa. Es la fórmula "everyday analogy" del canal.
- **Callback**: "which is what crushed the dummy" recupera la imagen de los 10 s.

## 4. Efectos y recursos

- **Muñeco de prueba aplastado** "como una lata" (gag visual literal del símil).
- **Luz que recorre el raíl** para señalar la zona peligrosa.
- Cartera vacía que se abre (gag de "cost him everything").
- Pancarta "OPENING TOMORROW", "GRAND OPENING", cartel "FIRST!" del rival y nombre del carro "UPSIDE-DOWNER".
- Torno de banco gigante para apretar el looping.
- Coche antiguo que da vueltas de campana en la analogía.
- **Explosión con hongo** al final.

---

## 5. Lo nuevo frente a los vídeos anteriores

1. **El villano provoca su propia derrota** al "mejorar" la obra del héroe.
2. **Analogía de coche** explícita antes de la física (la más didáctica de los 5).
3. **Final con explosión detrás del héroe sonriente** (como en Perfect Railroads): ya es un patrón de los vídeos de ingeniería.
4. El narrador se pregunta y se responde: "Does he know what he's doing? Not really."
