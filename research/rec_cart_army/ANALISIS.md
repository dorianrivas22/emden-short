## 1. Guion completo (Whisper large-v3)

> **He drilled 82 holes into the wood, because down the hill, an army is marching straight for his fort.**
> And while everyone else sharpens swords, he's in the corner still drilling.
> His commander asks, "Are we fighting them or grating cheese?"
> **But it gets weirder.** He bolts the wood onto a cart, and into every hole goes something long and thin, wrapped in paper, each with a string.
> **Then** he twists all the strings into one rope nobody's allowed to touch.
> One soldier reaches for it, and he slaps his hand so hard the whole fort hears it.
> **That night, it pours.** So he throws his coat over the cart, and lies on top of it like a human umbrella. "One drop gets in there, and we're all dead."
> **The next morning,** ladders slam against the walls, and the army charges.
> Everyone screams at him to do something, but he just watches them get closer and closer, until they're packed shoulder to shoulder, close enough to see their teeth.
> **That's when** he rips off the coat and lights the rope.
> The spark races down it and splits into 82 tiny flames, and each one hits a tube of gunpowder strapped to an arrow, turning every one of them into a rocket.
> **All 82 blast off the cart at once, flying every which way, but with the army packed that tight, not a single one misses.**

**Claves de escritura**
- **El número como gancho y como cierre**: "82 holes" al principio, "82 tiny flames" y "All 82" al final.
- **Ocultar el nombre del objeto** (nunca dice "rocket" hasta los 59 s): todo el vídeo es un "¿qué está construyendo?".
- **La espera como estrategia** (como el héroe de Perfect Railroads): el protagonista gana por esperar el momento exacto.
- **El defecto aparente se vuelve virtud**: los cohetes vuelan "every which way" (imprecisos), pero el enemigo está tan apretado que no importa. Es la explicación final.
- **Comedia**: "grating cheese", la bofetada que "the whole fort hears", "human umbrella".
- **3,54 palabras/s** (235 palabras, voz hasta 66,4 s), 4 pausas > 0,25 s (máx. 0,34 s). Sonoridad: **−14,1 LUFS, LRA 2,9 LU**.

## 4. Efectos y recursos

- Virutas de la broca; 82 flechas con tubos de papel; cuerdas que se trenzan.
- **Chorro rojo** de la bofetada y el soldado hundido en un agujero (exageración física).
- Lluvia con gotas, farolillos, abrigo como paraguas.
- **Chispa que corre por la mecha y se divide** (efecto de partículas doradas).
- Estelas de humo blanco de 82 cohetes y **hongo de explosión** final.
- Ejército enemigo amarillo y negro con estandartes de erizo: fácil de leer como "el otro".
