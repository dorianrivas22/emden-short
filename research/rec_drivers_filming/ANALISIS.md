## 1. Guion completo (Whisper large-v3)

> **He slammed the brakes on the highway, and the car behind him smashes right into his bumper.**
> He climbs out, holding his neck and limping, because he figures whoever hits you from behind always gets the blame.
> And sure enough, their insurance pays for his car, his doctor, and a nice fat check for all that pain he's in.
> **So, the next week, he does it again** with a different car.
> He picks them carefully. A guy on his phone, a kid who just got his license, anyone driving a little too close.
> **Then,** he brings a buddy, who drives right beside you so you can't swerve.
> The money comes in so fast, he buys a nicer car, just to crash that one too.
> **Eventually,** the insurance company starts calling. "Sir, this is your fifth neck injury this year." "Yeah, I've got a really weak neck."
> **But,** it's his word against the guy who hit him, and he's counting on everyone blaming the guy in the back.
> **Then, one rainy morning,** he picks a quiet guy in an old car, slams on his brakes, and out he comes, clutching his neck.
> **But the quiet guy doesn't yell, and he doesn't even get out.** He just points at a little box taped to his windshield.
> It's a camera, and it filmed an empty road. And a man braking for absolutely nothing.
> **And that's how the dash cam was invented.**

**Claves de escritura**
- **No hay "You see,"**: no hace falta explicar nada porque el giro es un objeto (la cámara). La explicación es la imagen.
- **El título es la pregunta, el final es la respuesta**: "Why drivers started filming the road" → "that's how the dash cam was invented". Estructura de origen ("cómo se inventó X") contada como historia de un pícaro.
- **El protagonista es el villano** y cae por la justicia poética (el estafador estafado por la prueba).
- **Listas de tres** por todas partes (coche/médico/cheque; móvil/novato/demasiado cerca).
- **El personaje "quiet guy"**: lo opuesto al ruido del estafador. Calla, no sale, solo señala.
- **3,86 palabras/s** (238 palabras, el más rápido de los 5) y 5 pausas > 0,25 s (máx. 0,34 s), casi todas al final para dar peso a la revelación. Sonoridad: **−14,0 LUFS, LRA 2,2 LU**.

## 4. Efectos y recursos

- **HUD de dashcam** (punto rojo REC, fecha 11/07/2026, velocidad en MPH que baja a 0).
- Lluvia con gotas en el cristal y **limpiaparabrisas** que barre el plano.
- Montañas de **fajos de billetes** que crecen y vuelan.
- Cartel "DAYS SINCE [ ] NECK INJURY" que cambia de 2 a 0 (gag de oficina).
- Camilla de hospital y collarín en el jardín; flotador de pato en la piscina.
- Humo y piezas que saltan en cada choque; gota de sudor en el estafador al ver la cámara.

## 5. Lo nuevo frente a los vídeos anteriores

1. **Historia de origen de un invento** ("that's how X was invented") con un pícaro como protagonista.
2. **Sin "You see,"**: el giro es un objeto que se enseña, no una explicación.
3. **Plano subjetivo de la propia cámara** (vídeo dentro del vídeo) como prueba final.
4. **Más cortes duros (10)** y **negros más profundos**: tono más de thriller.
