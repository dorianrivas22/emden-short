## 1. Guion (Whisper large-v3, por pasos)

> **You push a needle into cloth backwards, eye first, and pull it out. The thread stays behind, a loop hanging under the cloth. That loop is a stitch waiting to happen.**
> So you grind a point beside the eye and a hand wheel jabs it up and down… **but** nothing holds the loop…
> **So** a hook under the cloth catches each loop… *Linked like a chain, it's a chain stitch.*
> **However**, tug one loose end and the whole seam unravels. **So** you add a second thread underneath, wound on a spool inside a little boat… *A lock stitch, the stitch most machines still sew.*
> **But** the boat must stop and turn back… **So** a hook that spins takes its place… **Still**, each big loop… **So** a lever on top jerks up each stitch…
> **However**, sliding the cloth yourself makes some stitches long and some short. **So** little teeth rise… *They bite and hold like a dog's jaw, so they're called feed dogs.*
> **But** each time the needle lifts… **So** a foot on a spring presses the cloth down…
> **Unfortunately**, one hand still cranks the wheel… **So** your feet rock a plate below… *That plate is a treadle, from an old word for a step.*
> **But** at the end of each push, the wheel can stall… **So** the rod turns a heavy iron wheel…
> **Rock the treadle. The wheel spins, the needle dives, the hook swings a loop round the bobbin, the lever snaps it tight, and the teeth step the cloth.**
> A curved black arm closes over the wheel… **You pushed one needle in backwards and ended up with a sewing machine. Congratulations! You've just invented the sewing machine.**
> This is Episode 7 of the Invention Series. **Follow for more, because if…**
