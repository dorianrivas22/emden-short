## 1. Guion (Whisper large-v3)

> **He pushed a classmate into a dry well and laughed while watching him screaming, falling 80 feet and clawing at the wall. And the next day, the body disappeared.**
> So he threw a rabbit to test it out, and it was gone too.
> And he lived a normal life, forgetting about that incident, until 10 years later while fighting his wife because he pushed her into that same well and she vanished too.
> So he lowered himself into the well with a rope and he set up two hidden cameras to figure out what was going on.
> And a few days later, when his boss humiliated him in front of everyone, he lured him to his house and didn't hesitate.
> The body was gone by morning, so he checked the security cameras and they were pointing at him inside his office.
> So he lowered himself down that well again and found a hidden hatch… tunnels stretching for hundreds of meters… it was connected to his house.
> The next day, he asked his mother about it, but she felt sick, throwing up multiple times, so he got angry and walked her to the backyard. "I'm doing this for you." And he pushed her in that same well, then went inside to make coffee.
> **The next morning, she was still there.** The day after that, the smell started spreading through the neighborhood and he fled the state.
> **But why did it stop working?**
