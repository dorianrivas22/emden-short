# Project notes for Claude

The owner makes vertical animated story shorts (YouTube Shorts style) and talks in Spanish. Reply in Spanish.

## What this repo is
- **The Fake Chimney** (SMS Emden, Penang 1914): a finished short in English (`EmdenShort`, 57 s) and Spanish (`EmdenShortES`, 66 s). See `README.md` for files and render commands.
- `research/`: frame-by-frame study of the top shorts of the reference channel (style study only, never copy their stories, characters or shots; never name the channel in a video). One `ANALISIS.md` per video plus key frames, 0.1 s contact sheets, per-frame image metrics (CSV) and word-timed transcripts.
  - `01_ancient_jar` (33 M views, first 21 s), `03_job_japan` (17 M, first 25 s), `04_storm_oil` (16 M, complete, original English audio), `08_grave_bell` (12 M, complete, original English audio).
  - The 5 latest (Nov 2026), complete, 30 fps, original audio: `02_perfect_railroads` (28.7 M), `rec_roller_coaster` (10.5 M), `rec_pink_cell` (10.1 M), `rec_cart_army` (4.4 M), `rec_drivers_filming` (4.1 M). **`research/GUIA_ESTILO_CANAL.md` merges all 9 analyses into one style guide (structure by %, voice targets, transition catalogue with frame counts, light/colour targets, effects, captions, checklist). Read it before writing or storyboarding a new short.**
  - Other growing channels (Nov 2026), complete, 30 fps: `research/otros_canales/` (Stumblework Studios: invention series in 2nd person; Beggydone: dark 2D greyscale crime stories). Growth data in `research/CANALES_EN_CRECIMIENTO.md`; comparison in section 9 of the guide.
  - **The last 20 videos (Nov 2026), complete, 30 fps, measured in numbers: `research/GUIA_NUMERICA_20.md` (targets as median [p10-p90] for script, camera, light, colour, shadows, detail, characters, plus a pre-publish checklist). Read it before making a video and measure the render against it with `scripts/metricas_avanzadas.py` + `scripts/resumen_20.py`.** Data: `research/ultimos20/` (TABLAS.md, resumen.json, 1-fps sheets); re-run with `bash scripts/analizar_20.sh`.
  - Our first renders (Sewers, Rats, Money Stove) fall far outside the channel on camera motion (2-5x too still), detail (colours per frame 80-117 vs 285), saturation (0.58-0.69 vs 0.46), shadow hue (60-112 deg vs 238 deg blue) and screen time of faces (26-32 % vs 64 %). The owner called the last one "horrible": too little detail, unrealistic backgrounds, uninteresting characters.
  - Pipeline: `scripts/analizar_short.py VIDEO.mp4 research/NN_name` (30 fps metrics CSV, 30 fps contact sheets, 2 fps review sheets, 30 fps sheets around each transition, key frames, spectrogram, Whisper large-v3 word timings, `datos.json`); `scripts/bloques.py research/NN_name 0 3.5 9 …` prints block averages. Needs ffmpeg and the venv at `../.venv-analisis` (faster-whisper on the GPU). Source videos go in `research/_fuentes/` (not committed).

## The channel formula (from 9 videos; full detail in research/GUIA_ESTILO_CANAL.md)
- **Length**: 62-67 s in the latest videos (58-64 s before); 215-240 words.
- **Script**: one-line hook with an odd, concrete action and often an exact number ("every 39 feet", "82 holes"), no date, no name -> what is at stake in one sentence -> skeptics mock him with a funny line of direct speech -> escalation with *But / Then / So* plus a strange rule or taboo and 2-3 planted details -> a false failure or obstacle (~50 %) -> a 2-3 word hinge where the narrator voices the viewer ("Wait, what?", "But it's too late.") -> the explanation at 65-85 %: **"You see,"** + an everyday analogy that calls back an early line, OR explained live while it happens, OR shown only in the picture -> poetic justice / double twist / "fine print" ending that stops dead on the last word. Callbacks for everything planted; one number repeated through the story; three-step escalations to show time passing. Third person mostly, second person works.
- **Voice**: 3.5-3.9 words/s, 2-5 pauses > 0.25 s per video (max ~0.35 s), -14 LUFS, LRA 1.7-3.2 LU, a continuous bed ~4 dB under the voice, low hits on transitions and explosions.
- **Picture**: 2D bean-head white-faced characters with plum ink outlines in a detailed 3D toon world with black ink lines; a new visual idea every ~1.5-2 s; 0-3 hard cuts (the most viewed has 2): push-ins, whip pans, object wipes (a train or newspaper crossing the lens, 6-8 frames), 5-8 frame dissolves for time jumps, shape match cuts, flying through windows/roofs, crane moves from local to huge, the camera following a guiding object (rope, oil, spark), a jump reframe for punchlines. Always one "diagram" shot of the mechanism (now a macro with light/colour running along it) and one POV shot for suspense. Final shot: the protagonist in front, the result (often an explosion) behind. A literal visual gag on every noun; props with text carry the data.
- **Light/colour**: brightness 0.40-0.50, contrast 0.18-0.23, lifted blacks p2 0.08-0.14, saturation 0.35-0.55. Colour temperature follows the emotion (warm safe / cold danger / warm explanation); one hero colour = the key object, the only accent in cold scenes; purple-pink dusk when someone schemes, blue night for sabotage, pink dawn for the outcome; time told with light changing inside a shot; visible warm light sources in cold nights; 2-tone toon shading with coloured (never black) shadows.
- **Captions**: 1-2 words at a time, bold white sans with a thin black outline and soft shadow, centred at ~68-70 % of the height, numbers as digits.

## The v2 engine ("kit", Nov 2026) — use it for every new short
- `src/videos/kit/`: `Stage.tsx` (soft lighting with shadows: warm key + cool hemisphere fill, N8AO ambient occlusion, ink, lens blur, bloom, grade), `look.ts` (light/grade presets fitted to the guide), `camera.ts` (`live()`: continuous push/pull, lateral truck, orbit, roll — the channel's camera is never still), `tex.ts` (detailed painted textures), `mat.ts` (soft materials, glow, light shafts), `props.ts` (prop library: shelves, barrels, lamps with light, windows, labels with text, facades), `cast.ts` (channel-style characters: white heads, `sticks: true` ink limbs, silhouette overlays: toque, top hat, bowler, cap, moustaches, bow tie).
- First video on it: `src/videos/money2/` (`Money2Short`, "The Money Stove" v2, 63.9 s, captions on). Measured with `scripts/metricas_avanzadas.py`: 25 of 32 metrics inside the channel's p10-p90; the rest are borderline (highlights slightly warm, colours/frame 257 vs 269, zoom-in share 68 % vs 59 %).
- Calibration scene: `KitTest` (`src/videos/money2/Test.tsx`). Render a full video with `npx remotion render src/index.ts <Comp> out/... --codec h264 --crf 14 --gl=angle --concurrency=4` (~10 min for 64 s on the RTX 3060).

## How the Emden short is built
- `src/videos/emden/shots.ts`: every shot = time span (s, English timeline), mood, camera keys (`path`), and a `pose` that places ships/sets/characters. Characters' faces are at ~1.5 m; ships are 60 m long, so frame them lengthwise for vertical video.
- The Spanish version time-warps the English picture timeline to its own voice through `public/warp_es.json` (matched sentence and key-word starts); `audio/make_audio.py` warps the SFX the same way (`LANG_SHORT=es`).
- Character drawing, ink outline, lens blur: reused from the `cartoon-3d-short` skill template (`src/videos/bridge/`). The skill lives at https://github.com/mromidev/claude-animated-shorts (clone it into `~/.claude/skills/cartoon-3d-short/`; `audio/make_audio.py` imports its audio kit from there).
- Voices: Piper TTS, `en_US-ryan-high` (length-scale 0.86) and `es_ES-davefx-medium` (0.8). Spelling tricks for the TTS: "Penán", "Emmden", "mil novecientos catorce".
- Final mix: `audio/mix*_final.wav` = limiter + loudnorm to -14.5 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
- In the cloud (no GPU) a 1080x1920 frame took ~3.5 s; on a machine with a GPU render with Remotion's default GL.
